# About

I'm Rob Carroll — a writer who also makes websites. Yorkshire-born, now in the Peak District, via Australia, the UAE and the Netherlands.

## The writing

I'm working on my first novel, currently in its second draft. My short fiction won the Guardian short story competition. I've written journalism for The Guardian, The National, The Wire and Time Out — pieces on everything from mirror neurons to Syrian monasteries, including writing about music in Yemen and Syria — and, for a while, a parenting column. I studied jazz and American literature at Cambridge, which turns out to be decent training for both halves of this site.

## The websites

I've been making things for the web since starting an e-learning company in 2001. These days I run a small design and development studio called We Are Thought Fox. Along the way I led the team that rebuilt the website for the International Criminal Court and helped launch a national newspaper in Abu Dhabi.

## The rest

- Father to three children, including a World Para swimming medalist
- Played guitar in a few bands
- Ran a club night in East London
- Built a campervan
- Circumnavigated Australia
- Lifelong surfer (holding steady)
