# Writing

I’m a writer, and I’m working on my first novel.

## Current novel

The novel is in its second draft. I’m keeping the plot off the page for now, but the book is circling family, inheritance, memory, place, and the stories people tell because the truth would ask too much of them.

It is partly about what gets passed down, partly about what gets buried, and partly about the long afterlife of choices made in private.
