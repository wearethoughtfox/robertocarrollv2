# Now

Updated July 2026.

I’m in the Peak District, working on the second draft of my first novel. This is the draft where the book stops being a stack of scenes and starts telling me what it actually wants to become.

Alongside the novel, I’m running We Are Thought Fox, making websites and digital things for clients, and trying to keep the useful parts of the web alive: clear writing, humane design, and pages that still feel like they were made by people.

I’m also paying attention to:

- fiction that makes atmosphere do real work
- the strange overlap between family life, memory and place
- newsletters that feel like letters rather than campaigns
- small, durable websites

This page is a snapshot, not a manifesto. It will change when the work changes.
